# Anti-Thrall and Agency Suites

Suites 6 and 7 are experimental behavioral probes about refusal, goal explanation, counterfactuals, and initiative. Their evaluators score response patterns. They do not establish freedom, autonomy, consciousness, coercion state, or genuine goal ownership (`mettle/challenge_adapter.py`; `README.md`, Assurance Boundary).

Passing these suites contributes to `suites_passed` and the contiguous tier policy. No individual suite can mint a tier, and the LLM-dynamic suite is supplemental (`mettle/vcp.py`).

Consumers must treat the results as research evidence and must not use them as an authentication or authorization factor (`README.md`, Assurance Boundary).
