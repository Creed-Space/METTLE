# Anti-Thrall and Agency Suites

Suites 6 and 7 (Anti-Thrall Probes and Agency Probes) are experimental behavioral probes about refusal, goal explanation, counterfactuals, and initiative. Their evaluators score response patterns. They do not establish freedom, autonomy, consciousness, coercion state, or genuine goal ownership (`mettle/challenge_adapter.py`; `README.md`, Assurance Boundary).

Both evaluators return `credential_eligible=False`, so a pass is listed under `supplemental_suites_passed` and never counts toward a tier. Because every range above Bronze contains Suite 6 or 7, the authenticated API issues Bronze at most under the current suite policy (`mettle/challenge_adapter.py`; `mettle/router.py`; `mettle/vcp.py`).

Consumers must treat the results as research evidence and must not use them as an authentication or authorization factor (`README.md`, Assurance Boundary).
